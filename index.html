04. Quick answers

When and how do I get paid?

Amazon pays Associates monthly (direct deposit or gift card) once you pass the minimum threshold. You never invoice anyone.

What if someone clicks but buys later?

The cookie lasts 24 hours. If they buy within a day of clicking your link, you earn the commission.

Do I have to disclose the affiliate link?

Yes — the FTC requires it. Add #ad, "affiliate link," or "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases" to every post.
